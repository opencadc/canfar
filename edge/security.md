# Security

Please report security vulnerabilities privately rather than opening a public
issue. Email `shiny.brar@nrc-cnrc.gc.ca` with a description, affected version,
reproduction steps, and any relevant logs or proof of concept. Do not include
secrets or personal data in the report.

We will acknowledge reports and coordinate a fix and disclosure timeline. Keep
CANFAR and its dependencies up to date while a report is being assessed.

See the repository's [security policy](https://github.com/opencadc/canfar/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
for the canonical contact and policy text.
