# Community workflows

Ask questions, share feedback, and follow the roadmap in
[CANFAR Discussions](https://github.com/opencadc/canfar/discussions).
Read [Get help and shape CANFAR](guidelines.md) for where to post and how
maintainers follow up. Short reports and contributions beyond code are welcome.

These pages collect domain-specific workflows that build on the general
[Sessions](../sessions/index.md), [Storage](../storage/index.md), and
[Container Images](../containers/index.md) guides.

- [ALMA analysis workflow](alma/index.md): a text-first reduction and
  inspection path.
- [CASA and adjacent software](CASA_and_more.md): image selection and links
  to upstream package documentation.

Share a workflow in [Show and tell](https://github.com/opencadc/canfar/discussions/categories/show-and-tell)
or contact [support@canfar.net](mailto:support@canfar.net).
